Getting started

Klipt is signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens with a single click.

1

Download and move to Applications

Download the DMG above, open it, and drag Klipt into your Applications folder.

2

Grant Accessibility permission

Klipt needs Accessibility access to paste items with your keyboard shortcut. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and enable Klipt.

3

You're set

Klipt lives in your menu bar. Press ⌘⌥V to open it anytime. Everything you copy is automatically saved.

Klipt keeps everything on your Mac — no account, no telemetry, nothing uploaded. Updates arrive automatically.
