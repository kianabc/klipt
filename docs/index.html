How it works

  1. Copy anything, anywhere — text, an image, a screenshot, a file.
  2. Press ⌘⌥V to open the tray wherever you are.
  3. Pick a clip with the arrow keys and hit return. It pastes straight in.

space previews a clip without leaving the keyboard, P pins it, ⌫ removes it, and typing searches. Double-click a file to open it, or hold ⌥ to reveal it in Finder.

What it does

Text, images, files

Everything you copy goes into Klipt and stays there — all in one tray, not scattered across apps.

Screenshots, captured

Screenshots land in Klipt instead of cluttering your Desktop.

Pin what matters

Pin the clips you reach for often. Pinned items never expire and stay at the top.

Drag in, drag out

Drag files in to stash them. Drag them out to drop them anywhere. Multiple files stay grouped and move as a batch.

Preview without opening

Press space on any clip for a full Quick Look preview — images, PDFs, documents, video and audio.

Lock on screen

Keep Klipt visible to reference a screenshot or an address while you type in another app.

Search your clips

Find any copied text, file or screenshot instantly. Just start typing.

Keyboard-first

Browse, paste, pin, preview, expand and search without ever touching the mouse.

Your clipboard stays on your Mac

Klipt keeps everything locally. Nothing you copy is uploaded, synced or sent anywhere.

No account, no telemetry, nothing collected. History older than your chosen window is deleted automatically — pinned items are the exception, and they stay until you remove them.

Installing

  1. Download the .dmg above.
  2. Double-click it and drag Klipt to your Applications folder.
  3. Open it. Klipt lives in your menu bar, not the Dock.
  4. Grant Accessibility access when asked — that's what lets Klipt paste for you.

Signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens with a single click. No right-click workaround, no security warnings to work around.
